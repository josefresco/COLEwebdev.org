Why it matters

An unmaintained WordPress site is a liability waiting to happen.

WordPress powers 43% of the web, which makes it the most-targeted platform on the internet. The attacks are automated and relentless — outdated plugins don't get a grace period.

Monthly maintenance isn't a luxury reserved for large companies. It's the responsible baseline for any site that accepts contact forms, processes payments, or shows up in search results.

The cost of skipping it

What happens when maintenance gets deferred.

These aren't edge cases. They're the four most common outcomes we clean up for businesses who let maintenance lapse.

✦
Security Breaches
Unpatched plugins are the number-one attack vector. A hacked site can redirect visitors, expose customer data, and take days to clean up — if you catch it at all.
◇
Broken Functionality
WordPress, plugins, and PHP version mismatches accumulate over time. What worked last year quietly stops working — often at the worst possible moment.
⚙
Plugin Conflicts
Skipping updates means skipping compatibility fixes. Eventually a forced update cascades into a broken checkout, contact form, or gallery.
↗
SEO Penalties from Downtime
Search engines demote sites that go down or serve errors. A single security incident can erase months of ranking progress.
