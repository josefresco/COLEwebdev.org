Why it matters

What a strong website does for salons & spas.

Cape Cod beauty businesses serve two distinct client bases: the year-round local who books by relationship and habit, and the summer visitor who searches Google on a Tuesday looking for a haircut before a weekend event. A professional salon or spa website captures both. We build websites for Cape Cod hair salons, day spas, nail studios, and wellness businesses that load fast, show your work clearly, and make booking easy from any device.

◇

Clients decide based on what they see

Beauty is a visual decision. A potential client looking for a new salon is comparing portfolios before they compare prices. A website that shows your work, your team, and your atmosphere clearly wins the comparison before any conversation happens.

◷

Online booking reduces no-shows and front desk volume

Clients who book online show up. A well-configured booking system sends automatic confirmations and reminders, reduces no-shows measurably, and lets clients book after hours without calling. That translates directly to a fuller appointment book with less phone time.

✦

Summer visitors are a second client base worth capturing

Seasonal visitors on Cape Cod need hair appointments, spa days, and nail services just like locals do. A site that ranks for searches like "hair salon Wellfleet" or "day spa Chatham" captures that intent from visitors who are ready to book right now.

What we build

Built for how salons & spas actually operate.

Online Booking Integration

Vagaro, Fresha, Mindbody, or Square Appointments embedded directly in your site so clients book and pay without calling. Works on every device, 24 hours a day.

Portfolio & Before-After Gallery

Hair color, cut, nail art, and treatment results displayed in fast-loading galleries that show potential clients exactly what to expect.

Services & Pricing

A clear, organized menu of services with pricing so clients arrive informed and appointments run on time. Easy to update when pricing changes.

Staff & Stylist Profiles

Individual profiles for each stylist or therapist with their specialty, experience, and portfolio. Helps clients book the right person, not just the next available slot.

Gift Card & Retail Sales
