Is it time?

Signs your website needs a redesign.

If any of these sound familiar, your current site is costing you business — not just looking old.

↗
Slow load times

If your site takes more than 3 seconds to load, visitors leave — and Google drops your rankings. Speed is no longer optional.

◇
Not mobile-friendly

Over 60% of Cape Cod searches happen on phones. If your site doesn't work well on mobile, you're invisible to most of your market.

✦
Looks dated

First impressions happen in under a second. An old-looking site signals that your business is behind — even when it isn't.

◎
Poor conversions

Visitors land but don't call, don't fill out forms, don't buy. The design isn't guiding them to take action.

✎
Hard to update

If every text change requires calling your old developer, you've lost control of your own business presence.

⚙
Not showing up in search

Old sites often have weak technical SEO — missing meta tags, no schema markup, poor page structure. A rebuild fixes all of it.

Redesign vs. New Build

Different project. Different process.

A redesign isn't just a fresh coat of paint. You're coming in with years of content, an existing URL structure, and real search rankings. We treat those as assets, not obstacles.

  • Content to migrateYou've built up years of copy, photography, and pages. We audit everything, decide what to keep, and move it cleanly to the new site.
  • Redirects requiredEvery URL that changes needs a 301 redirect. Miss one and you lose the SEO equity that page has accumulated over the years.
  • SEO equity to protectYour existing rankings are an asset. We map your current URL structure before we touch anything and protect every page that drives traffic.
  • Brand continuityA redesign doesn't mean starting from zero. We keep what's working — your colors, your logo, your voice — and modernize everything else.

Our process

From audit to launch — every step accounted for.

01
Site Audit

We crawl your existing site, catalog every URL, check page speed, review rankings, and identify what to keep, improve, or retire.

02
Define Goals

What do you need the new site to do differently? We get specific about conversion goals, audience, and the problems the redesign has to solve.

03
Design

Mockups built from scratch — not from a template. We present for review, refine with your feedback, and finalize before a single line of code is written.

04
Build

Custom development against your approved designs. Mobile-first, fast, and built to the technical SEO standards your site needs.

05
Migrate Content

Copy, images, testimonials, and existing pages moved cleanly. We edit and improve along the way — not just copy-paste.

06
Redirect Setup

301 redirects configured for every URL that's changing. Google keeps giving your new pages credit for the work your old ones did.

07
Launch

We handle the DNS switch, verify all redirects, run a final checklist, and stay available after go-live for anything that comes up.
