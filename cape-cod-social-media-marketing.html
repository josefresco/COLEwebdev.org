Platforms

The right channels for your audience.

We don't push you onto every platform — we focus on the ones where your customers actually spend time.

◍

Facebook

Still the dominant platform for Cape Cod locals. Events, community groups, and local recommendations all happen here. Essential for businesses serving year-round residents and the seasonal summer crowd.

◇

Instagram

The visual showcase for Cape Cod's natural beauty, food, and lifestyle. Ideal for restaurants, shops, lodging, and any business whose work looks good — which on the Cape, is most of them.

✦

LinkedIn

The right channel for B2B services, contractors, professionals, and nonprofits looking to build credibility and reach decision-makers rather than the general public.

◎

Google Business

Not a social network, but your Google Business Profile posts and Q&A appear in search and Maps results. Keeping it active signals to Google — and customers — that your business is current and responsive.

Why it matters

Social media and Cape Cod are a natural fit.

Cape Cod businesses operate in one of the most visually compelling, community-driven markets in New England. The combination of seasonal tourism, tight-knit year-round communities, and a landscape people love to photograph makes social media uniquely effective here.

The challenge is consistency. Most small business owners know they should be posting — and start strong in May — but by July the feed goes quiet because running the actual business takes over. That's where a managed service pays for itself.

COLEwebdev has built websites and run marketing for Cape Cod businesses since 2000. We know the seasonal calendar, the local audiences, and what kinds of content perform here — because we live and work in this market too.

↗
Seasonal reach

Cape Cod's summer population swells to over 500,000. Tourists plan trips months in advance and follow local businesses on social to decide where to eat, shop, and stay.

◍
Local community trust

Year-round residents choose businesses they see showing up consistently in the community. An active, authentic social presence signals you are here to stay — not just here for the season.

◇
Visual storytelling

The Cape's landscape, food, and lifestyle are inherently photogenic. Social media is the natural medium for the kind of place-based storytelling that builds loyalty and word-of-mouth.

◎
Paid precision

Facebook and Instagram ads let you target by ZIP code, interest, and even visitor behavior — reaching exactly the right people at the right moment in the Cape Cod travel cycle.
