Platforms

WooCommerce or Shopify — we'll help you choose.

WooCommerce
Built on WordPress, WooCommerce is the right choice when you want full ownership of your store — your server, your data, your code. We recommend it for businesses that already have (or want) a WordPress site and need flexibility.
  • Seamlessly integrated with your WordPress site
  • Full control over design and checkout flow
  • No monthly platform fees beyond hosting
  • Hundreds of extensions for subscriptions, bookings, and more
  • Ideal for physical goods, digital downloads, and services
Best for: WordPress sites · Custom requirements · Full ownership
Shopify
A hosted platform built specifically for selling. Shopify handles security, hosting, and PCI compliance so you can focus entirely on products and customers. We recommend it for businesses that want a proven, low-maintenance storefront.
  • Hosted and maintained by Shopify — nothing to update
  • Built-in abandoned cart recovery and marketing tools
  • Point-of-sale integration for brick-and-mortar shops
  • Extensive app store for any feature you need
  • Ideal for product-focused businesses with large catalogs
Best for: Product-first stores · Retail · Low maintenance
